Name: p-Aminobenzoic acid (PABA) (CAS # 150-13-0)
SMILES: O=C(O)c1ccc(N)cc1
Info: PABA is a biochemical intermediate bacteria and fungi use to synthesize folate (an essential B vitamin). Derivatives of PABA, such as sulfonamide drugs, are ubiquitous antibiotics due to their structural similarity and ability to interfere in the biosynthesis of PABA.
